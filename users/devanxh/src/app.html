    By the numbers

    Leadership Principle stories

    Every round in the loop carries two behavioural questions, and the Bar Raiser will push three levels deeper than you expect. Write one real story per principle in STAR form — situation, task, action, result, and what you would do differently. Keep the setup under thirty seconds, own the "I" not the "we", and land on a number. Two principles are marked lower priority: they come up rarely at entry and mid level, so write them last.

    How the hiring bar works

    Interviewers are each assigned a slice of the principles and write up notes against them before debriefing together. The Bar Raiser is from outside the hiring team, has veto power, and is asking one question: are you better than the median person already in this role. That is why a technically correct SQL answer with a thin story still fails, and why every answer wants a metric attached to it. Assume the loop is five to six rounds, that you will be asked to write SQL live, and that at least one round is a design conversation with no single right answer.
